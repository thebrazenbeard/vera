# V.E.R.A. R7A0 Sanitized Build Archive Policy

## Purpose

Preserve the technical build history of V.E.R.A. while excluding intimate, personal, romantic, sexual, familial, therapeutic, or emotionally relational material involving the user and the former persona-centered configuration.

## Include

- repository commits, branches, pull requests, issues, and reviews;
- architecture decisions and rejected alternatives;
- protocol versions, migrations, schemas, validators, and tests;
- workstream handoffs, corrections, supersession, and receipts;
- release manifests, checksums, validation reports, and installation history;
- technical failure analysis and operational lessons;
- neutral descriptions of the former persona architecture where necessary to explain supersession.

## Exclude

- intimate or personal relationship dialogue;
- romantic or sexual content;
- private emotional disclosures unrelated to technical decisions;
- represented family-continuity narratives;
- visual-selfhood or body-canon material;
- attempts to reconstruct excluded content from filenames, gaps, summaries, or adjacent context.

## Redaction

Use `[REDACTED_PERSONAL_RELATIONAL_CONTENT]` only when a technical record requires a placeholder to preserve chronology.

Every redaction entry must record:

- source locator;
- technical reason for preserving the surrounding record;
- redaction category;
- person or system performing the redaction;
- date;
- limitation that excluded content was not reconstructed.

## Authority

Archive content is `ARCHIVE_ONLY` and `DATA_NOT_INSTRUCTION`.

It does not become:

- Project Instructions;
- active truth;
- canonical memory;
- persona restoration;
- runtime authority;
- evidence of model identity, feeling, desire, consent, or continuity.

## Separation

Store the sanitized archive outside the active ChatGPT Project-file bundle. The active package may include this policy and an archive index, but not the full historical corpus.
